Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how M.Awaji LiveScores handles information when you visit our website for live football scores, fixtures, results, standings and match details.

Information processed when you visit

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. As part of delivering a website, the hosting platform and internet services may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, request information and security logs according to their own policies. We do not ask visitors to provide personal information to view scores.

Football data and third-party services

The website requests football information from a connected third-party football-data API. When your browser loads scores or match details, requests may be sent to that provider. The provider may process technical request data under its own privacy policy. Football data can be delayed, incomplete or corrected by the provider.

Some website features, such as theme preferences or favourites, may use browser storage on your device if enabled by the current version of the website. You can clear website data through your browser settings. Clearing it may reset saved preferences or favourites.

This policy should be updated if advertising or analytics services are added or changed. We do not claim that AdSense is currently active on this website.

Cookies and your choices

Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies and clear stored website data. Some functions may not work as expected if you disable storage or block requests. Where required by law or provider policy, appropriate consent and privacy choices will be provided before relevant advertising or tracking technologies are used.

Third-party websites and policies

GitHub Pages and the football-data provider operate under their own terms and privacy notices. If advertising or analytics providers are added, their applicable privacy notices will also be relevant. Please review those notices for details about their processing practices.

Children's privacy

M.Awaji LiveScores is a general football-information website and is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter.
